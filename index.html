QAOAscheduling

A Reproducible QUBO–QAOA Benchmark for Generalized Bipartite Assignment on NISQ Hardware

This repository contains the research results for QI26-20 / QIntern 2026 (QWorld). It models generalized bipartite assignment as a Quadratic Unconstrained Binary Optimization (QUBO) problem and benchmarks classical baselines, standard Quantum Approximate Optimization Algorithm (QAOA), and Grover-Mixer QAOA (GM-QAOA) in ideal simulation and on real IBM Quantum hardware.

Core Findings & Scope
  • Not Quantum Advantage: Establishes a controlled correctness and transpilation resource benchmark on small NISQ instances, not a runtime competition against classical exact solvers.
  • Classical Baselines: Evaluated via exact enumeration, the Hungarian algorithm, greedy methods, and simulated annealing across 3×3 and 4×4 one-to-one, and 4×2 exact-capacity instances.
  • Standard QAOA vs. GM-QAOA: While GM-QAOA achieved 100% ideal feasible-subspace concentration in simulation, physical execution on ibm_marrakesh revealed that GM-QAOA's high transpilation overhead (~7.9× more native CZ gates than standard QAOA) led to severe distribution degradation (TVD 0.881). Both physical circuits successfully sampled the global optimum, but high-probability recovery was constrained by physical circuit depth.
